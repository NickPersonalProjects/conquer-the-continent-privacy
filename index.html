Conquer the Continent Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Conquer the Continent is an offline, single-player game. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or gameplay data with the developer or third parties.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, settings, unlocks, resources, best times, and suspended expeditions locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the game and is not transmitted to the developer.

Save import and export

If you choose to export a save, Android's system document interface asks you where to store the file. If you choose to import a save, you select the file through Android's system interface. These actions are initiated by you and do not send the file to the developer.

Permissions and network access

The app does not request internet, advertising identifier, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or broad file-storage permissions. It has no advertising, analytics, account system, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Exported save files remain in the location you selected and can be deleted with your device's file manager.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The intended Google Play target audience must be selected accurately by the publisher before release.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before the changed version is released.
